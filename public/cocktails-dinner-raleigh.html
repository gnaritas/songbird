Evenings · from 4 PM

Cocktails, natural wine, and a dinner worth staying for

The bar program reads the same way the kitchen does: by season, by farm, by what is in front of us tonight.

The cocktail program

Ingredient-driven drinks built around the season. Zero-proof, low, and full-strength all sit on the same list, and every drink prints its ABV — you will never have to guess what you are in for.

The wine list

A natural-leaning list alongside the cocktails, poured through the evening.

Small plates

Seasonal plates made for sharing: produce from farms like Goodness Grove, plus the fermented and preserved things the kitchen put by for moments like this.

Four-course prix fixe

The full dinner — four courses that read like the moment, fermentation, preservation, and foraged greens included.

The room

Just over 40 seats, plus a patio. A mural by Dain Kim runs along the wall, drinks land on ceramics from Piedmont Clay Works, and the big cedar communal table is made from reclaimed wood.
