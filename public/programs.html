Programs · East End Market

Two programs, one room

Mornings belong to coffee, juice, and the oven. Evenings belong to the bar. Same tables, same people, two very different lights.

Mornings — 8 AM to 2 PM, daily

Willow House Coffee, cold-pressed juice, seasonal smoothies, Tin Roof Teas blends, matcha from Frame, and pastries and morning plates from neighborhood makers. Walk-up window for grab-and-go.

See the morning program →

Evenings — from 4 PM

Seasonal cocktails with ABV printed on every drink, a natural-leaning wine list, small plates for sharing, and a four-course prix fixe dinner. Sun–Wed till 10, Thu–Sat till midnight.

See the evening program →

The clock behind it all

Every part of the menu tracks the Piedmont’s micro-seasons — fermentation, preservation, and foraged greens included. What pours and what plates changes as the moment changes; the hours and the two-program shape stay put.
