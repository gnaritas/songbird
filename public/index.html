Daylight to dusk.Coffee to cocktails.

Songbird is a bar that keeps two clocks. Mornings are bright: coffee roasted a few blocks away, pressed juice, tea, matcha, and something from the oven. After 4, the same room turns into a full bar — seasonal cocktails, natural wine, and dinner till late.

The morning program

Bright, seven days a week

8 AM to 2 PM, every day. Walk in, or grab it at the walk-up window on your way past.

Coffee

Espresso and brewed coffee, roasted a few blocks away by Willow House Coffee.

Juice and smoothies

Cold-pressed juice and seasonal smoothies, blended from Piedmont fruit as it turns.

Tea and matcha

Custom tea blends from Tin Roof Teas, plus matcha drinks from Frame.

From the oven

Pastries and morning plates from makers around the neighborhood.

The evening program

The same room, after four

From 4 o’clock Songbird turns into a full bar: cocktails built on the season, a natural-leaning wine list, and dinner — just over 40 seats, plus a patio.

Cocktails

Ingredient-driven drinks built around the season — zero-proof, low, and full-strength, with the ABV printed on every drink.

Natural wine

A natural-leaning list for slow evenings.

Small plates

Seasonal plates for sharing — produce from farms like Goodness Grove, plus things the kitchen fermented and put by.

Prix fixe dinner

A four-course dinner that reads like the moment: fermentation, preservation, foraged greens.

Built on the season

Menus that follow the moment

Songbird was built around seasonality and locality. The menus shift as the Piedmont’s micro-seasons shift — produce from farms like Goodness Grove, things the kitchen fermented and preserved, and foraged greens when they show up. The drink list moves on the same clock.
