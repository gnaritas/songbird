Mornings · 8 AM–2 PM · every day

Coffee, juice, tea, and something from the oven

No reservations needed in the morning. Walk in, or order at the walk-up window on the East End Market side.

The coffee

Espresso and brewed coffee from beans roasted close by on South Saunders Street — Willow House Coffee is the roaster, a few blocks out. Morning cups lean on those local beans.

Pressed and blended

Cold-pressed juice and seasonal smoothies, blended from Piedmont fruit as it turns. What is on the list changes with the moment.

Tea and matcha

Custom tea blends made for Songbird by Tin Roof Teas, plus matcha drinks built with tea from Frame — hot or iced.

From the oven

Pastries and morning plates from makers around the neighborhood, set out through the morning.

The walk-up window

In a hurry? The morning program runs a walk-up window for grab-and-go at East End Market.
