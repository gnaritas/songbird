Privacy

What this site keeps

The contact form

When you send a note, we collect your name, your email (optional phone), and your message. It is used for one thing: answering you. It is not sold, and it is not added to a marketing list.

Cookies and trackers

This site sets no cookies and runs no third-party analytics or advertising scripts. The map on the visit page is a Google Maps embed, which may set its own cookies when you interact with it.

Your note, your call

Sent us something and want it gone? Leave a note in the contact form asking us to delete it, and we will remove it from the inbox it landed in.

Questions

Use the contact form for anything privacy-related.
